## Overview

GiveCare is built on one insight: the healthcare system tracks the patient; nobody tracks the caregiver. Our brand voice is that of someone who has walked the caregiving path — not a clinician, not a brand manager. Warm, direct, specific. We validate first, always. We give people steps they can follow when they are exhausted.

The content engine has five pillars: caregiver wellbeing (3x/week), invisible labor (weekly stats/policy), practical navigation (2x/week how-to), crisis support (real-time, as needed), and community proof (weekly caregiver stories). The rule for all of it: name the human moment before the product.

Safety gates are strict by design. Caregiving touches grief, burnout, end-of-life, and crisis. Anything in those zones routes to human review. Everything else can be autonomous at 0.85+ rubric score.
